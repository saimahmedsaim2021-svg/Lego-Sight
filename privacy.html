Privacy Policy for Lego Sight

Last Updated: Loading...

1. Information We Collect

At Lego Sight, we prioritize your security. When you register or interact on our live sports platform, we collect information including:

  • Your Name and Email address via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Your comments, likes, and profile preferences submitted during live matches.
  • Device and browser information for optimal 16:9 video player streaming performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly to enhance your sports viewing experience:

  • To verify your identity when posting real-time comments, liking, or subscribing to matches.
  • To provide seamless adaptive streaming across Smart TVs, computers, and mobile devices.
  • To protect our community against spam and unauthorized broadcast interruptions.

3. Security & Cloud Protection

All user authentication tokens and database entries are stored securely using Google Cloud Firebase infrastructure. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any third-party advertisers.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, contact our official support team via the contact details provided in the footer.
